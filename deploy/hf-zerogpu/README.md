# NutriAssist-RW

Nutrition advice for caregivers of children under 5 in Rwanda, in English and Kinyarwanda, with a meal
planner built from local foods. Source code: https://github.com/pauline12ish34/NutriAssist_Capstone

Not a substitute for a health worker. In an emergency, go to the nearest health centre or call 912.
